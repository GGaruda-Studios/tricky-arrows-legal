Tricky Arrows

Privacy Policy

Google Play package pastry.trickyarrows.tap.puzzle
Published by Golden Garuda Studios

Last updated 19 September 2026.

The short version

Tricky Arrows collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no way to reach the internet. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. Everything the app knows about you stays on your device.

What the app stores

One file on your device, holding only what the game needs to work:

  • which puzzles you have completed, and the puzzle you are part-way through
  • your daily-challenge streak and the date of your last win
  • your settings: sound, music, vibration, guideline, zen mode, theme, and reduced motion
  • whether you have seen the tutorial

That file is private to the app. Android removes it when you uninstall Tricky Arrows. Deleting the app's data from Android Settings erases it immediately.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, transmit or sell personal information.
  • It does not use advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporting or any tracking technology.
  • It does not request the INTERNET permission, so it is not technically capable of sending your data anywhere. The only permission it requests is VIBRATE, used for the small haptic taps when you remove an arrow.

If you email us

The Settings screen has a Send feedback link. Tapping it opens your own email app with a blank message addressed to us, and adds three lines at the bottom: the app version, your device model, and your Android version. Nothing is sent unless you write the message and press send yourself, and you can delete those lines first if you prefer.

The app itself sends nothing — it hands the draft to your email app and stops there. If you do send us a message, we will see your email address and whatever you wrote, and we use it only to answer you.

Backups

Tricky Arrows leaves Android's standard Auto Backup switched on, so that reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone does not cost you your progress. When Auto Backup is enabled on your device, Android copies the save file described above into your own Google Drive account, in a private area that counts against neither your Drive storage nor our access — we cannot read it. That copy is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy, and you can turn Auto Backup off at any time in Android Settings under Google → Backup.

Children

Tricky Arrows is a puzzle game suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If a future version of Tricky Arrows ever collects or transmits anything, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the app's release notes on Google Play.
